About

Behind the camera

I'm Bilal Aamer — a photographer based in the UAE. I'm drawn to honest moments: the laughter between poses, the quiet glance before the ceremony, the family chaos that makes a house a home.

My approach blends calm, documentary-style coverage with gently directed portraits, finished with warm, true-to-tone edits that will still feel right twenty years from now. I shoot weddings and celebrations, corporate functions, and automobile and outdoor sessions across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the wider Emirates.
